About us

A crown over
Broadway since 1961

Queen's Bakery — 皇后餅家 — has stood at 809 North Broadway, in the heart of Los Angeles Chinatown, since 1961. Six decades of almond cookies, rice puffs and birthday cakes, baked steps from where they're sold.

Today the counter still opens at 8:30 every morning: breads and tarts fresh, cakes made to order, and boxes of rice puffs travelling to families all over the country. Specialty breads and desserts, fresh fruit filled cakes for any occasion — call to order.
